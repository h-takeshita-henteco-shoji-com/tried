## 3. Actions and results
I judged that promises 1 and 2 could not both be met this month. Building the 56 more cemeteries needed for 55,000 jobs would have dropped money below the starting 498,704. Following the rule "when in doubt, pick what raises the weekly balance most", I went for promises 1 and 3.

1. **Cut service budgets and turned off EducationBoost:**
   - Electricity, water and roads went to 80%, healthcare to 60%, education to 80%, and fire and police to 100%.
   - Spending outside healthcare fell 19,356 a week. No power or water outages.
   - **Missed prediction:** I expected about −21,400 from these. Road spending did not move at all.
2. **Raised taxes by 2 points on commercial, industrial and offices:** income rose 10,014 a week against a forecast of about 12,000.
3. **Tore down the 238 cemeteries in the southwest and switched off 7 monuments:**
   - Healthcare and monuments together fell 37,048 a week.
   - Jobs fell 53,325 → 46,136 the next day, then 45,474 by month end.
   - **Missed prediction:** dead-body problem flags rose 221 → 405, and low-land-value flags rose 19 → 562.

All three together took the weekly balance from −26,789 to +46,439, beating my forecast of +44,000.

## 4. Promises at the end of term
1. **Weekly balance +40,000 or more:** kept, at +46,439.
2. **Jobs 55,000 or more:** not kept, at 45,474 (starting point 43,075).
3. **Money no lower than at the start:** kept at every monthly review. It ended at 940,710.

- **Several operations counted as one action:** I counted the 7 budget changes plus the policy change as one action, and the 4 tax changes as one. Each change stayed within the allowed limits.
